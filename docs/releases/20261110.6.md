# Neoism v0.7.149-nightly.20261110.6

The sixth Rune nightly carries everything prepared for nightly `.5`, which was never published on every platform, and one fix made after it.

## Changes

- The model, agent and effort chosen in one agent tab or workspace no longer leak into another: each keeps its own composer settings.
- Closing a native window, a workspace tab or an SSH/server join releases its workspace connection. Terminals close before the workspace is released, SSH tunnels stay up until the close is acknowledged or times out, and a dropped client stops retrying.
- Background sub-agents stream and finish correctly, and thinking stays before its own response while streaming.
- The model, agent and effort chips come back when switching workspaces.
- A table's scroll bar stays above the input bar and shows anywhere over the table, including after dragging it.
- Compaction shows a green "Compacting…" line without a progress bar and a centered divider; background tasks show a red loader in the sidebar.
- A history refresh with repeated or missing message ids no longer crashes, including compaction error notices.
- Closing the last tab of a conversation family releases its stream subscription and cached history.

## Validation

The Rune app's 313 tests pass at this commit. The changes listed for `.5` were validated as described in its notes (`20261110.5.md`); actual SSH transport shutdown has not been exercised against an external SSH host. The macOS and Windows builds are gated by CI only.
