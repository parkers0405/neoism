When the user describes a concrete malfunction and asks why it happens or asks to check it out, they expect investigation followed by a fix, not a diagnosis-only final answer. In the closed-chat-tab/subagent sidebar bug, stopping after a correct root-cause explanation prompted: "okay why did you not fix it brother, fix it". Unless they explicitly request explanation/review only, carry the bug through implementation and verification without waiting for a second request.
