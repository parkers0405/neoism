# Neoism v0.7.149-nightly.20261111.1

This Rune nightly follows `20261110.6`.

## Changes

- Coming back to a conversation (from another tab, a sub-agent or another workspace) shows it as it stands at once. Before, everything that had arrived meanwhile played back, the view scrolling down after it for as long as the text had taken to stream. A conversation left at its end comes back at its end; one left scrolled up comes back where it was.
- A title too long for its tab or row slides to show the rest while the pointer is on it: pane tabs, workspace tabs, and conversation and sub-agent rows in the sidebar. It rests 0.6 s, travels to its end and back, and the ellipsis returns when the pointer leaves.
- A sub-agent's reply no longer streams with every word doubled and its markdown broken. Viewing a sub-agent made the daemon bind a second event stream to it beside its conversation's; a sub-agent whose conversation is followed now gets none of its own. A sub-agent at work also renders its reply as a streaming one.
- Closing a window no longer waits up to two seconds on each workspace's cleanup acknowledgement: the wait happens after the window is gone.

## Validation

The Rune app's 318 tests pass at this commit. The return to a conversation was measured in a real window: a 4 s reply streamed while another tab showed took 4.4 s to play back before and lands on the first frame now. A traced real sub-agent run, opened while it streamed, went from 594 repeated deltas in 3,640 to 1 in 3,070. The sliding title was seen on a pane tab; the sidebar and workspace-tab titles use the same code and were not looked at separately. The window-close change is covered by its unit test only. The macOS and Windows builds are gated by CI only.
