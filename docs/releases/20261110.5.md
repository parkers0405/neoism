# Neoism v0.7.149-nightly.20261110.5

Rune now releases workspace connections when closing a native window, workspace tab, or SSH/server join. Terminal closes precede workspace release, SSH tunnels remain available until acknowledgement or timeout, and disconnected clients stop retrying once dropped. Shared server workspaces remain subject to the daemon ownership checks.

- Fix background sub-agent streaming and completion state, and keep thinking before its own response while streaming.
- Restore model, agent, and effort chips when switching workspaces.
- Keep table scrollbars above the composer and visible anywhere over the table, including after dragging.
- Show green “Compacting…” activity without a progress bar, a centered compaction divider, and red sidebar loaders for background tasks.
- Prevent history-refresh crashes from repeated or missing message IDs, including compaction error notices.
- Release agent-tab stream subscriptions and cached histories when the last tab for their family closes.

Validation includes the Rune unit suite, a WebSocket close/acknowledgement test, headless scrollbar and compaction renders, and a native window-close check that returned daemon workspace registrations to their original count. Actual SSH transport shutdown has not been exercised against an external SSH host.
